## 1. Bolt.new (StackBlitz): no affiliate program, referral only

- Affiliate or sponsorship program: **none**. Bolt's support docs say: "Bolt doesn't currently have affiliate or sponsorship programs." Source: https://support.bolt.new/account-and-subscription/corporate-commercial
- What exists: a personal **Refer a friend** program, run through **Cello** (not Impact, PartnerStack or Rewardful). Source: https://support.bolt.new/account/referral-program
  - Reward: 15% of what the referred user pays Bolt, capped at **$50 per referral**. Paid subscriptions only, not free sign-ups.
  - Referred user gets 20% off (first 3 months on monthly, or once on annual), applied automatically, no code.
  - Held 30 days before it's credited (refund window). Cello reviews for fraud. No self-referral. No reward for someone joining your existing Teams plan.
  - Payout method: **PayPal or Venmo**, connected in Bolt under "Earn $50" > Rewards > Add payment details.
- Signup URL: there's no application. You log in to bolt.new with a **personal** account, then click "Earn $50" in the sidebar to get the invite link.
- Requirements: a Bolt personal account. That means a new Bolt login for UseThisSI.
- Fit: low to medium. Bolt is an Alt pick, not a Top pick. Payout is capped at $50, and it pays to PayPal/Venmo rather than a bank form. It's framed as "refer a friend", and the docs don't explicitly cover publishing the link on a directory.
- Recommendation: **don't apply now.** Revisit only if Bolt traffic shows up in analytics. Doing it needs a new Bolt account, and Baz has to decide whether PayPal/Venmo is OK for payouts.

## Why this order
- ElevenLabs is first: it's a Top pick, the rate is the best documented (22% recurring for a year), signup is instant, and the minimum is $5.
- Make is second: Top pick and 35% for 12 months. But the payout gate (3 paying referrals and $100) is slow at our size, and payouts go through Wise.
- Gamma is third: 25% for 12 months, but it's an Alt pick and needs approval.
- n8n is fourth: Top pick at 30%, but only Cloud counts, the PayPal threshold is EUR 100, and much of our audience self-hosts.
- Runway is fifth: a flat $15 is small, but the 25% discount code helps readers, it pays to a bank via Stripe, and the free Max plan is useful. Our X account counts as a channel.
