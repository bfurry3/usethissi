## The big one
### Gemini adds a hub for small business owners (Oct 20)
**What happened:** Google launched a business landing page inside Gemini. You can save your brand style (website, brand kit, fonts, colors) and Gemini reuses it for visuals made with Nano Banana 2.1. It also turns messy spreadsheets and receipts into charts and simple models.
**Why it matters for builders:** Solo operators get one place for brand assets, numbers and customer insights. Gemini now connects to Google Business Profile, Google Ads and Workspace, plus Adobe, Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, Wix, Zoho and Xero.
**The catch:** Google's post does not say which plans or countries get the business page. And a chart built from a pile of receipts is only as good as the receipts, so check totals against your books before you act on them.
**How to try it:** Open [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com), upload your brand kit once, then ask for a launch graphic. Compare the plans on our [Gemini page](https://www.usethissi.com/tools/gemini-deep-research/pricing/) first.
[Read the source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/new-tools-small-business-owners/)

## Tool of the day
### Beacon
**Use it for:** Sharing what one coding agent learned with the next one. It records sessions from Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenCode and 20+ other harnesses and turns fixes and corrections into reusable skills.
**Price:** Open source (MIT) and free to self-host. Beacon Cloud has a free plan, and Cloud Pro is $20/mo billed annually.
**Why we like it:** If you switch between agents, you stop re-explaining the same project quirks. The open-source version keeps session data on your own machines. Asymptote Labs announced a $5.5M seed round for it on Oct 20.
[Try it on UseThisSI](https://www.usethissi.com/tools/beacon/)

## One tip to try
**Keep a lessons file for your agents:** Add `LESSONS.md` to your repo and point your agent's instructions file at it. At the end of each session, ask: `Add one line to LESSONS.md: what went wrong today and the fix.` Next session starts with last week's mistakes already known.
