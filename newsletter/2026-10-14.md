## What shipped this week

1. **Gemini 4 Argon is out (Oct 13).** Google's new frontier model for long coding and agent work, with a 1M-token limit. It costs $2 in / $10 out per 1M tokens until Nov 30, then doubles to $4 / $20.
   *Try this:* run your nastiest refactor through it in Google AI Studio before the intro price ends. [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/)
2. **Claude Haiku 5.5 (Oct 7).** $0.10 in / $0.50 out per 1M tokens for prompts up to 100k, about 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5, and it scores 72.4% on OSWorld computer use. Max and Team subscribers also get a new monthly API credit.
   *Try this:* use it as the cheap subagent for summaries and lookups in your agent stack. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)
3. **GPT-6 with Intelligent UI (Oct 7).** ChatGPT now replies with interactive UIs (charts, forms, mini tools), and the Free and Go tiers get it too.
   *Try this:* ask it to "build me a calculator for X" and see what comes back. [Source](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/)
4. **Meta Muse Wonder (Oct 12).** New agent models with "Active Memory," available through the Meta Model API and Muse Code. [Source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-wonder)
5. **Google Cloud's Gemini agent (Oct 8)** and **AWS's open-source Physical AI Toolchain (Oct 8)**, for enterprise agents and robotics. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-at-work/) · [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/aws-launches-open-source-physical-ai-toolchain-for-robotics/)
