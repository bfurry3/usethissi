■  The big one

Builder.io open-sources Agent-Native (beta, Oct 14)

What happened: Builder.io released Agent-Native, an open-source TypeScript framework for agentic apps. The agent and the UI share the same actions, data and state.

Why it matters for builders: Define a capability once and it shows up in the UI, HTTP API, CLI, MCP and A2A. You can connect Slack or Notion over MCP, or call your app from Cursor, Claude or ChatGPT.

How to try it: Clone BuilderIO/agent-native and run a sample app (Clips, Slides or Design).
